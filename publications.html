TAG-X: Joint Multispectral Representations for Molecular Structure Elucidation

L. J. Vasseur, R. Gautam, M. Gfeller, J. Barozet-Golbery, R. Schlama, P. Schwaller

NeurIPS 2026 workshop on Agentic Systems for Molecular Sciences (ML4Molecules), Paris · December 2026

Accepted workshop paper · First author

step2point: Optimising point-cloud representations for fast calorimeter simulation

A. Zaborowska, P. McKeown, P. Raikwar, M. Mozzanica, L. J. Vasseur, M. Casadei, M. Casiraghi, S. Diallo, S. Chopra, T. Tej Nandi

CHEP 2026, Bangkok · 26 May 2026

Oral presentation by Anna Zaborowska · Co-author

Preparing detailed particle-detector simulations for machine learning by compressing energy deposits into smaller point clouds while preserving relevant physical quantities. This CERN-led work addresses the trade-off between the cost of a representation and the physics it retains.
